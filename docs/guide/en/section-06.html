6. Processing and FX

Expand a strip for EQ, Gate, Dynamics, inserts and its other processing pages. Apply a model to display its controls. Tilt EQ is on the EQ page; High-cut is on Home.

Expand Effects for an FX slot. Apply Model, then open Parameters. Parameters is hidden while Model is None. Parameter pages follow the selected model. Insert routing is configured on the strip's Insert page; FX model and wet/dry controls are on the relevant FX pages. Available models and controls follow the selected slot. Use chapter 12 to save or load a complete processor preset.

When connected, choose a preset on the VSS3 Parameters page and press Enter to load it. Press Enter again to reload the same preset.

Tap tempo: open the FX slot's Home, Parameters or Routing page and tap Alt+T at least twice. The first press starts timing; each later press applies the interval since the previous press. Holding the key does not produce more taps. After a pause longer than four seconds, the next press starts again. Only the current slot changes, within its supported range.

Supported models: DELAY/REVERB, STEREO DELAY, ULTRATAP DELAY, TAPE DELAY, STEREO CHORUS, STEREO FLANGER, TREMOLO/PANNER, PHASER and MOOD FILTER. Other models report that tap tempo is unavailable and keep their values.
