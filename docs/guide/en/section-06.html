6. Processing and FX

Expand a strip for EQ, Gate, Dynamics, inserts and its other processing pages. Choose a model and press Enter to display its controls. Changing a model may make a previously focused parameter unavailable. Bypass does not remove its settings. Tilt EQ is on the EQ page; High-cut is on Home.

Expand Effects for an FX slot. Choose Model with Enter, then open Parameters. Parameter pages follow the selected model. Insert routing is configured on the strip's Insert page; FX model and wet/dry controls are on the relevant FX pages. Available models and controls follow the selected slot. Use chapter 12 to save or load a complete processor preset.
