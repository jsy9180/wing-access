12. Channel and FX presets

On a strip or FX page, choose File > Channel and FX presets > Save WING channel or FX preset.... The file extension follows the target: .chn, .aux, .bus, .main, .mtx or .fx.

Load WING channel or FX preset... opens destination and Scope selection. Strip presets stay within their family. Insert 1/2 selects where included FX will load; None leaves that insert disabled. Only compatible FX slots are offered. Review Scope before loading, including any feeds or connections you want to include.
