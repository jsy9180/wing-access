9. Read and record meters

Read current meter

Alt+M reads the focused meter immediately. On another control, it reads the related meter: for example, input on Config, output by Fader, or gain reduction in Dynamics. It does not open a page or start recording. Press it again on the same meter within two seconds to hear numbers only. A pause or page change restores the context.

Gate/Dynamics reads the latest value followed by the strongest reduction received in the last 200 ms. Stereo readings use left then right. There is no continuous automatic speech. Waiting for meter data / Meter data unavailable identifies a missing reading. Offline meters display Offline.

Choose an individual meter under METERS to focus just that reading. Stereo meters have Left and Right children. FX meters are under METERS > FX processors; related meters also appear beside processing controls. Unverified units are labelled raw. RTA spectrum readings are not provided.

Record a period

On an individual meter page, check Record meter to start and clear it to stop. You may navigate elsewhere while it runs. Afterwards, read Minimum, Average, Maximum and, on level meters, Meter RMS. Recording status includes sample count and receive gaps. Recording time shows hours, minutes and seconds in one control.

To record a group, select any hierarchy in the tree and choose Metering > Start meter recording. For example, METERS > Channels > Channel 1 includes all its meters, even collapsed children. Metering > Stop meter recording stops all records under that hierarchy. Existing child recordings keep their original start and results when you start the parent, but stop with it. Meter group pages also have Start recording and Stop recording buttons. These actions can be assigned keys.

Results remain in memory until replaced by another recording or the app closes. Disconnect stops recording and retains results; reconnect does not restart it. Recording controls are unavailable offline. A model change stops records whose meaning changes. Receive gaps are excluded from averages. Meter RMS is calculated from received levels; this feature does not record audio or measure LUFS/true peak. Live Alt+M readings and the 200 ms peak remain separate from recording.
