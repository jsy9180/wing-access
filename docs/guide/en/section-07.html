7. Mix, Sends and Faders

Open a strip's Bus Sends, Main Sends or Matrix Sends to edit its outgoing paths. Select Send on, Send level and the available Send mode/tap controls there.

A destination strip's Mix pages group incoming sources in banks, beginning with Channels 1–16. Each source has Send on and Send level. Expand an individual source for its additional settings.

Faders groups strip Faders and Mute controls independently of a destination Mix. Use Alt+M to read the relevant meter on Mix and Faders. Standard layer positions follow the page rules in chapter 4; user layers always jump directly to their registered targets.
