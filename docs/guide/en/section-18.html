18. Credits

WING Access is an independently developed application based on the published WING control protocols. The following projects and publications informed its implementation and interaction design.

Developed by Seongyyeol Jang. Free for individual users; redistribution is not permitted. See terms of use. Third-party licenses apply to their respective components.

WING documentation

Behringer's WING series user manual and Patrick-Gilles Maillot's WING Remote Protocols provided the console and protocol references. Patrick-Gilles Maillot's WING tools and wapi examples provided additional protocol examples and development tools.

Libraries and accessibility

Danny Firesnake's libwing supplies the native protocol and discovery library, adapted for WING Access. The offline catalog also comes from libwing. See the included third-party notices for revisions, local changes and dependency license information.

AZ Accessible OSC (azaosc) and MXAccess32 inspired the keyboard navigation, page organization and accessible control workflow. NVDA by NV Access and Sense Reader by XVision Technology provided screen-reader keyboard and accessibility references.

Additional references

Mixing Station's WING documentation, WING source-tag documentation, Arbor Live's show-file project and wing-snap-types informed compatibility and file-format work. OSARA provided an accessible-meter workflow reference.
